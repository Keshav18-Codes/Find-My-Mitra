Demo with made-up people. Drag the red pin, no real locations are used.

You switch it on

Sharing is off until you turn it on, and you can turn it off at any time.

Only approved friends

A friend sees you only after you accept their request. Nobody else can.

One tap for help

SOS sends your current location to your approved friends as a notification.

Start in three steps

  1. Add your friends

    Search by name or email and send a request. They accept it from their Friends page.

  2. Turn on sharing

    Open your dashboard and allow location access in your browser.

  3. Open the live map

    Friends who are sharing appear as pins. Select one to zoom to them.
